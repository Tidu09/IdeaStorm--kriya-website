Shipping and Delivery Policy

At Kriya, we are committed to delivering your STEM kits promptly and reliably. This Shipping and Delivery Policy outlines our shipping practices and procedures.

Delivery Timeframes

Orders are typically processed within 1–2 business days. Delivery times vary based on location:

  • Metro Cities: 3–5 business days
  • Other Regions: 5–7 business days

Shipping Charges

We offer free shipping on all orders across India. No additional shipping charges apply.

Order Tracking

Once your order is dispatched, you will receive a tracking ID via email and SMS. You can use this ID to monitor your shipment's progress on our tracking page.

Courier Partners

We collaborate with trusted courier services, including Delhivery, Bluedart, and Ecom Express, to ensure timely and safe deliveries.

International Shipping

Currently, we do not offer international shipping. Our services are limited to addresses within India.

Delivery Delays

While we strive for timely deliveries, unforeseen circumstances like weather conditions or logistical challenges may cause delays. We appreciate your understanding in such cases.
