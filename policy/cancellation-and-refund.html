Cancellation and Refund Policy

At Kriya, we strive to offer the best experience to our customers. If you wish to cancel your subscription or order, please review the terms below:

Cancellation Terms

You may request a cancellation of your subscription at any time. However, the cost of any kits already dispatched or in transit will be deducted from your refund.

Refund Policy

Refunds will be processed after deducting the price of delivered kits (if any) and a standard handling charge of ₹1000.

Please allow 5–7 business days for the refund to reflect in your original payment method.
